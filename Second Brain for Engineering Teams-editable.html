Field Issue Case
Every report gets an isolated case—not an automatic update to global knowledge.
Component knowledge contains reviewed, reusable guidance—not every field hypothesis.
•
Retrieve validated diagnostics during investigation.
•
Preserve provenance, owner, and reviewed date.
•
Never promote a suspected cause during triage.
"STAGE-119 has an approved settle-time diagnostic procedure."
"Stage vibration caused this failure" — hypothesis, not shared truth.
Component Knowledge
Curated with boundaries. Retrieve freely; promote only after review.
